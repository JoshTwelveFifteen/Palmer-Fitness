About me

Strong body.
Strong mind.

Strength, rehab and confidence — built around you.

Ten years ago I swapped the world of media for something more hands on and rewarding.

I am driven to help every sort of person feel stronger, in body and mind. I have a particular focus on injury rehab and helping people rebuild confidence in what their bodies can do.

Every programme is built around you. Your goals, your starting point, your life. There's no one-size-fits-all plan and no exercise-as-punishment: just steady, encouraging progress, with plenty of hard work and lots of laughter along the way.

I train from my private studio in Tottenham, working with a small number of clients at a time, so sessions stay personal, relaxed and genuinely enjoyable.

If this sounds like the thing you're looking for, please get in touch. I'd love to hear from you!
